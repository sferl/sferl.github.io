The course *Cryptography and Cryptanalysis* is a course offered in the master's degrees of Mathematics and of Computer Science TUM (Munich), led by [Lorenz Panny](https://yx7.cc/). In this course, the students have to solve weekly CTF-style cryptanalysis challenges. I hold weekly tutorials, presenting the challenges and helping the students solve them.
